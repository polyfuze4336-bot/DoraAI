# DORA Presentation Guide

A suggested slide flow with speaking notes. Adapt to your time slot.

## Slide 1 — Title

**DORA: AI-Powered Commodity Decision Intelligence**
> One line: "Understand what's happening, why it matters, and what could happen next."

## Slide 2 — The Problem

Fragmented commodity signals; slow, manual, inconsistent analysis; limited and unexplainable prediction; risk tracked reactively. (See [business-value.md](business-value.md).)

## Slide 3 — The Idea

A continuous pipeline that combines prices, news, risk, market intelligence and manufacturing into one explainable management view.

## Slide 4 — What DORA Does

The eight-step loop: collect, normalise, detect change, forecast, reason (AI over evidence), score risk, recommend, report.

## Slide 5 — Live on Azure Today

- Deployed URL, scheduled ingestion, PostgreSQL + Blob, deterministic engines, Microsoft Foundry, Application Insights.
- Emphasise: deployed and running, not a mockup.

## Slide 6 — Architecture

Use the diagram from [../architecture/current-azure-architecture.md](../architecture/current-azure-architecture.md). Walk it with the [architecture-talk-track.md](architecture-talk-track.md).

## Slide 7 — Deterministic + Grounded AI

- Numbers are deterministic and reproducible.
- AI (Foundry) only explains retrieved evidence. This is the trust story.

## Slide 8 — Live vs Demo (honesty slide)

Show the [live-vs-demo.md](live-vs-demo.md) split. Judges trust teams that are precise about this.

## Slide 9 — Roadmap

Phase 1 (now) → Phase 2 (enterprise data + advanced intelligence) → Phase 3 (multi-agent + Copilot Studio). See [../roadmap/dora-roadmap.md](../roadmap/dora-roadmap.md).

## Slide 10 — Future: Copilot Studio

Explain the platform is architecture-ready to be exposed through Copilot Studio without rebuilding the Azure backend. See [../copilot-studio/copilot-studio-evolution.md](../copilot-studio/copilot-studio-evolution.md).

## Slide 11 — Close / Ask

Restate value; invite questions. Keep the [judge-questions.md](judge-questions.md) answers ready.

## Delivery Tips

- Lead with the live demo if time is tight; architecture second.
- Use plain business language; reserve service names for the architecture moment.
- Never overclaim. "Configured/architecture-ready" is a strength, not a weakness.
