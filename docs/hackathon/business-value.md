# Business Value

## The Problem in Business Terms

Commodity-exposed organisations lose time and money because commodity intelligence is fragmented, manual, slow and inconsistent:

- Analysts stitch together prices, news and economic data by hand.
- Management reports lag reality.
- Forecasts live in spreadsheets and can't explain themselves.
- Risk is noticed after it bites, not before.

## What DORA Changes

| Pain | DORA capability | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Fragmented sources | Continuous multi-source ingestion + canonical model | One place to see everything |
| Manual analysis | Deterministic forecasting, risk and scenario engines | Faster, consistent output |
| Slow reporting | Automated weekly commodity brief | Management-ready, on time |
| Unexplainable forecasts | Grounded AI explanations (Microsoft Foundry) | "Why" alongside "what" |
| Reactive risk | Continuous deterministic risk scoring | Earlier awareness |
| Inconsistent insight | Shared engines and evidence grounding | Repeatable answers |

## Why It's Credible

- **Deployed on Azure today**, not a slide.
- **Deterministic numbers** — reproducible and auditable.
- **Grounded AI** — explanations cite retrieved evidence.
- **Enterprise-ready design** — managed identity, Key Vault, IaC, CI/CD, observability.

## Value Narrative (for judges/executives)

> "DORA compresses the time from raw signal to management decision. Instead of analysts spending hours assembling a commodity picture, DORA maintains it continuously, forecasts deterministically, and explains itself in plain language — so leadership acts sooner with more confidence."

## Cost Posture (prototype)

DORA reuses existing platform resources (Container Apps environment, registry, Foundry, AI Search, monitoring) and adds a lean data/identity/messaging layer. Model usage is bounded to small-capacity deployments. This keeps the prototype inexpensive while proving the full workflow.

## Where the Value Grows

- **Phase 2:** enterprise + commercial data multiply the signal quality.
- **Phase 3:** multi-agent orchestration and Copilot Studio put DORA where executives already work (Microsoft 365), widening adoption without re-platforming.
