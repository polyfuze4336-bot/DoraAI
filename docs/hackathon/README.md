## One-Paragraph Pitch

DORA continuously combines commodity prices, global news, emerging risks, market intelligence and manufacturing signals to help management understand what is happening, why it matters and what could happen next. It is deployed on Azure today: it ingests live public commodity and news data on a schedule, normalises it into a canonical model, runs deterministic forecasting and risk scoring, and uses Microsoft Foundry to explain the findings in management language — all grounded in cited evidence.

## Presenter Rules (accuracy first)

1. Only call something "live" if it is on the [live list](live-vs-demo.md).
2. Call manufacturing and seeded records "demo data" out loud.
3. Every number comes from a deterministic engine; AI explains, it does not invent numbers.
4. Copilot Studio is a **future** direction, not a shipped feature.
